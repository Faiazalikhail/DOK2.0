# Sketches

Each physics mechanic needs a sketch showing how it works in the game. Photograph or scan the pages from the notebook and save them here with these names, so the links in the GDD resolve.

| File | Mechanic | What the sketch should show |
|---|---|---|
| `01-projectiles.png` | Kinematic projectiles | The arc from sling to target, a short draw against a full draw, and a growth aiming ahead of a moving player |
| `02-character-motion.png` | Force-based character motion | Speed against time rising to a top speed, and a jump arc with its height and distance marked |
| `03-oscillation.png` | Moving platforms | The platform between its two points, the amplitude marked, and the player riding it |
| `04-friction-drag.png` | Friction and drag | Speed dropping to half on entering the roots, and a slide losing speed at a steady rate |
| `05-two-bone-ik.png` | Two-bone IK | A leg as two bones on a step, the trace down, the foot target and the knee angle |

Hand-drawn is fine.
